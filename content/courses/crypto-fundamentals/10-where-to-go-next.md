You've built the core of a crypto education: what a
[blockchain](glossary/blockchain.md) is, how wallets and keys work, how to buy
and send safely, how [networks](glossary/consensus.md) run, and what
[DeFi](glossary/defi.md) actually is. Here's how to keep going without getting
scammed or overwhelmed.

## The learning map

**1. Vocabulary (build it daily)**
Browse the [glossary](glossary/README.md), one term a day. Every term links to
its neighbors, so ten minutes a day compounds fast.

**2. Practice on testnets**
Get comfortable with wallets, DEXs, and staking on
[testnets](glossary/testnet.md) before using real funds. Experience with zero
risk is the single best advantage you can give yourself.

**3. Verify everything**
Use [block explorers](glossary/block-explorer.md) to check transactions.
Investigate tokens before touching them. Read
[tokenomics](glossary/tokenomics.md) with a skeptic's eye.

**4. Deep-dive by interest**
- **Security:** the [self-custody](glossary/self-custody.md) and
  [cold wallet](glossary/cold-wallet.md) paths.
- **Investing logic:** [market cap](glossary/market-cap.md),
  [volatility](glossary/volatility.md), [tokenomics](glossary/tokenomics.md).
- **Technology:** smart contracts, [L2s](glossary/layer-2.md),
  [rollups](glossary/rollup.md), [ZK proofs](glossary/zero-knowledge-proof.md).
- **How it's really run:** take the **Decentralization Analyst** path — you'll
  learn to score a chain's distributed power the way W3D does.

## Habits that separate safe learners from victims

- Move slowly; verify identities out of band; never share keys.
- Only ever act when *you* initiated the contact.
- Learn how to read one block explorer and one token checker inside out.
- Play with amounts you can lose on anything new.
