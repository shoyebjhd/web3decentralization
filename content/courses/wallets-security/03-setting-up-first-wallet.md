Time to do it for real. This walkthrough takes about 15 minutes and ends with
a backed-up wallet holding a tiny test amount. Follow it in order — the
sequence is the security.

## Step 1 — Download from the real source

Go to the wallet's official site by typing the domain yourself (never via a
search ad or a link someone sent). Verify the URL twice. Install the app or
extension. If anything asks for money, personal documents, or an existing
seed phrase during *setup*, stop — that's not setup, that's theft.

## Step 2 — Create, don't import

Choose **Create new wallet**. The app shows 12–24 words: your
[seed phrase](glossary/seed-phrase.md). This is the moment everything depends
on.

## Step 3 — Back up offline, right now

1. Write the words on paper, numbered, in order. Twice (two copies).
2. Store the copies in two separate safe places.
3. Do NOT screenshot, photograph, email, or cloud-note them. Ever.
4. The app will quiz you on random words — pass it honestly.

## Step 4 — Fund with a test amount

Receive a tiny amount first (a few dollars). Confirm it arrives in the wallet.
Then — and only then — send a tiny amount *out* to prove you can. You have
now tested the full loop: in, backup, out.

## Step 5 — Lock it down

Set a strong device/app password (this encrypts the local copy — it does NOT
replace the seed phrase). Enable biometric lock if offered. Bookmark the real
site. Delete any screenshots you accidentally took.

## What you now have

A wallet only you control, a tested backup, and proof you can move funds both
ways. That puts you ahead of most crypto holders on day one.

> The rule you'll repeat forever: backup before balance. Keys first, coins
> second, always in that order.
