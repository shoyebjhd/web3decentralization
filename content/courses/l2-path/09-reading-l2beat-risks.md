**Objective:** turn [L2Beat](glossary/l2beat.md) from a scary dashboard into a 3-minute risk verdict for any L2.

## Concept: the three tabs that matter

1. **Risk:** who can upgrade, sequence, and freeze — names, thresholds, delays. This tab *is* the audit summary.
2. **TVL:** size of the honeypot. Big TVL + early stage = maximum caution per dollar.
3. **Activity:** real usage vs mercenary farming. Dead chains with big TVL are exit-liquidity museums.

## Hands-on lab (free)

1. Grade three L2s you know by stage (0/1/2) from memory, then check against L2Beat. Calibrate your instincts.
2. For one chain, trace a single risk line (e.g., "upgrade multisig 4/7") to the actual addresses and signers.
3. Write a one-paragraph risk verdict for a chain a friend uses — practice communicating risk plainly.

## Safety checklist

- Stage 0 + large deposits = custodian with extra steps. Size accordingly.
- Watch for *downgrades* (projects quietly loosening security) as closely as upgrades.
- L2Beat lags brand-new launches — brand-new + big incentives = maximum skepticism.
