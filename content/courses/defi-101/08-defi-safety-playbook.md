## Before any protocol touches your money

- [ ] Verified contracts + named team (or credible anon track record)?
- [ ] Audit exists, criticals fixed, code unchanged since?
- [ ] TVL real and distributed (not one whale + emissions)?
- [ ] Yield source explainable in one sentence?
- [ ] Oracle, admin keys, and upgrade powers identified?
- [ ] Bridge/custody assumptions listed and accepted?

Two "don't knows" = don't deposit.

## Position rules that survive crashes

- **Size by ruin:** no single protocol holds enough to end you. Beginners:
  cap experimental DeFi at money you can lose smiling.
- **Stablecoin base:** keep reserves in boring stables across two issuers;
  risk assets are for risk budgets.
- **Leverage is a profession:** spot first, always. If you touch perps or
  looping, that's a separate education with preselected tuition (your money).
- **Exit rehearsed:** know the unwind clicks (unloop, repay, withdraw) before
  the panic â€” cascades don't wait for tutorials.

## Ongoing hygiene

- Revoke stale token approvals quarterly.
- Separate wallets: vault (cold), operations (hot), experiments (disposable).
- Track everything for taxes from day one â€” explorers forget nothing, and
  neither do tax agencies.
- Re-read your positions monthly: rates, health factors, unlock calendars.
