# Ethereum Decentralization Audit

Ethereum is the programmable 'world computer' chain that launched smart contracts and DeFi. This audit scores its decentralization across four pillars —
infrastructure, capital, governance, and software — using the methodology in
[data/methodology.md](../../data/methodology.md).

**Composite decentralization score: 80.8 / 100** · *Best-in-class client diversity, lingering staking concentration risk*

## Four pillars

- **Infrastructure (30%)** — 84/100 · Nakamoto 3 · 8,200+ beacon nodes; relays still concentrated
- **Capital (25%)** — 72/100 · Nakamoto 4 · Lido + top CEX stakers control >40% of stake
- **Governance (25%)** — 80/100 · Nakamoto 0 · Off-chain rough consensus via EIPs / All-Core-Devs
- **Software (20%)** — 88/100 · Nakamoto 2 · Geth ~52%, Nethermind, Besu, Erigon, Reth

## What the score means

- Software (88): the industry's best example of client diversity — a single-client bug would not halt the chain.
- Capital (72): the weak pillar; Lido plus top exchange stakers exceed 40% of staked ETH, a real capture risk that the network tracks closely.
- Governance (80): mature off-chain process (EIPs, All-Core-Devs) that is slower than on-chain governance but resilient.
