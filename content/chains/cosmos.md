# Cosmos Decentralization Audit

Cosmos is the 'internet of blockchains' using the CometBFT (Tendermint) consensus. This audit scores its decentralization across four pillars —
infrastructure, capital, governance, and software — using the methodology in
[data/methodology.md](../../data/methodology.md).

**Composite decentralization score: 68 / 100** · *Battle-tested governance; small validator cap concentrates infra*

## Four pillars

- **Infrastructure (30%)** — 70/100 · Nakamoto 7 · 180-validator cap; bare-metal heavy operator base
- **Capital (25%)** — 58/100 · Nakamoto 8 · Top CEX and institutional stakers hold material voting power
- **Governance (25%)** — 78/100 · Nakamoto 0 · Active on-chain governance via Cosmos SDK
- **Software (20%)** — 65/100 · Nakamoto 1 · Gaia (Cosmos SDK + CometBFT); single dominant client

## What the score means

- Governance (78): battle-tested on-chain governance via the Cosmos SDK is genuinely active and functional.
- Infrastructure (70): a smaller validator cap concentrates infrastructure relative to Polkadot or Cardano.
- Capital (58): top CEX and institutional stakers hold material voting power — the key centralization vector.
