# Polkadot Decentralization Audit

Polkadot is a sharded multi-chain network designed to connect many specialist chains. This audit scores its decentralization across four pillars —
infrastructure, capital, governance, and software — using the methodology in
[data/methodology.md](../../data/methodology.md).

**Composite decentralization score: 72.6 / 100** · *Strong on-chain governance; validator set capped by NPoS design*

## Four pillars

- **Infrastructure (30%)** — 75/100 · Nakamoto 97 · 297 active validators, geographically diverse
- **Capital (25%)** — 64/100 · Nakamoto 9 · Web3 Foundation + early backers retain weight
- **Governance (25%)** — 82/100 · Nakamoto 0 · OpenGov on-chain referenda; one of the most active
- **Software (20%)** — 68/100 · Nakamoto 2 · Polkadot SDK / Substrate; multiple node implementations

## What the score means

- Governance (82): OpenGov delivers genuinely active on-chain referenda — among the most functional governance in crypto.
- Infrastructure (75): extremely hard for any party to capture, though the validator count is capped by the NPoS design (297).
- Capital (64): Web3 Foundation and early backers retain notable weight in token economics.
