# Cardano Decentralization Audit

Cardano is a peer-reviewed proof-of-stake chain built around slow, deliberate upgrades. This audit scores its decentralization across four pillars —
infrastructure, capital, governance, and software — using the methodology in
[data/methodology.md](../../data/methodology.md).

**Composite decentralization score: 70.4 / 100** · *High operator pluralism; single-client risk balances research-grade governance*

## Four pillars

- **Infrastructure (30%)** — 78/100 · Nakamoto 24 · ~3,000 stake pools, geographically distributed
- **Capital (25%)** — 68/100 · Nakamoto 31 · Stake delegation broad; IOG/Emurgo treasury notable
- **Governance (25%)** — 72/100 · Nakamoto 0 · CIP-1694 on-chain governance (Voltaire) rolling out
- **Software (20%)** — 60/100 · Nakamoto 1 · Cardano Node dominant; minimal client diversity

## What the score means

- Infrastructure (78): among the most distributed operator bases in crypto — hard for any party to compromise.
- Capital (68): stake delegation is broad, though treasury entities (IOG, Emurgo) retain notable weight.
- Software (60): the weak pillar — one dominant client (Cardano Node) means a severe bug could be systemic.
