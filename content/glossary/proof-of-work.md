Proof of Work (PoW) is the original consensus mechanism, used by Bitcoin:
miners race to find a hash below a difficulty target, spending real electricity
for every guess. The winner gets to propose the next block and earns a reward.
An attacker would need more computing power than the honest majority — usually
intractable.

## Why it matters

PoW's "pay to participate loudly and publicly" design makes historical chains
extremely hard to rewrite, which is why Bitcoin remains the decentralization
benchmark. Its costs are real electricity and hardware, which PoS was created
to replace.
