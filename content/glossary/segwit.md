SegWit (Segregated Witness, 2017) moved transaction signatures out of
Bitcoin's [block](glossary/block.md) size limit — effectively raising capacity
without a hard [fork](glossary/fork.md) — and fixed transaction malleability,
which unblocked the Lightning Network and cleaner second layers.

## Why it matters

SegWit was Bitcoin's block-size war settlement: bigger blocks via accounting
change instead of rule break. The lesson for analysts: Bitcoin upgrades by
soft fork and rough consensus, slowly — which is exactly why its governance
scores so highly, and why every change takes years of argument first.
