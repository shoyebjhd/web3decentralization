FOMO — fear of missing out — is buying because the price is rising and
everyone's getting rich except you. It's the emotion market tops are made of:
green candles disable risk assessment exactly when risk is highest.

## Why it matters

Nearly every beginner loss story starts with FOMO entry — buying the top of a
parabolic move, then panic-selling the retrace. The antidotes are mechanical,
not motivational: pre-set entry prices, position limits, and a rule against
buying anything up 50%+ in a week without a week's wait. Systems beat feelings.
