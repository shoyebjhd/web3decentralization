A fair launch means no insiders got tokens first — no presale, no VC
allocation, no team premine. Distribution happens through open
[mining](glossary/mining.md), public farming, or [airdrops](glossary/airdrop.md)
anyone could join. Bitcoin is the archetype; most launches since are not.

## Why it matters

"Fair" is crypto's most diluted adjective — projects claim it while founders
hold 30% via "ecosystem funds." The verifiable version is on-chain: check
genesis distribution, first-month holder spread, and whether insiders'
wallets were funded before the public could participate. Fairness you can't
audit is marketing.
