A crypto-backed stablecoin (DAI is the standard) holds its peg with
on-chain [collateral](glossary/collateral.md) instead of bank dollars:
lock $170 of ETH, mint $100 of DAI. If collateral value falls toward the
loan, automatic [liquidation](glossary/liquidation.md) sells it to protect
the peg — no humans, no banks involved.

## Why it matters

It's the decentralization-respecting stable design — transparent reserves
anyone can audit live, no freezer switch on ordinary holders. The price is
capital inefficiency and reflexive risk: in a market crash, mass liquidations
can strain the peg exactly when stability matters most. Know which backing
your "dollars" rely on before you need them to hold.
