A limit order buys or sells only at your price or better — "buy ETH at $3,000
or lower" — waiting in the [order book](glossary/order-book.md) until matched.
The opposite is a market order: fill immediately at whatever price exists,
bleeding [slippage](glossary/slippage.md) on thin books.

## Why it matters

Limit orders are how you refuse to donate to volatility: set your price,
walk away, let the market come to you. On DEXs, limit functionality (or
tight-range positions) similarly protects against sandwiching your own market
buy. Amateurs market-buy breakouts; professionals leave limits and wait.
