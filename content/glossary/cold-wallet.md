A cold wallet stores your private keys on a device that is **offline** — most
commonly a dedicated hardware wallet, but paper or an offline computer count
too. Because it never touches the internet, it can't be drained by remote
hackers.

## Why it matters

The safest way to hold meaningful amounts of long-term crypto is cold storage.
"[Not your keys, not your coins](https://web3decentralization.com/what-is-a-seed-phrase/)" is
practical when a hardware wallet keeps those keys in your hands, offline,
immune to phishing and malware.
