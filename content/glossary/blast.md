# Blast

Blast is an Ethereum Layer-2 that launched with native yield on ETH and
stablecoins, moving the "restaking + yield" model into the rollup itself.
On the W3D Terminal its decentralization score is **still pending** — the
terminal's four-pillar audit (infrastructure, capital, governance, software)
for this chain has not been locked yet, so no `/100` is published.

**What the two pillars we track say so far:** infrastructure and capital are
the sensitive ones for a yield-native rollup — the sequencer runs on an
operator stack (concentration risk under the "software" pillar), and native
yield bridged into the proof-of-stake ecosystem adds a capital-dependency
that an L1 proposal doesn't carrycher. When the score lands, the biggest
moving parts will be sequencer operator count, who actually controls the
upgrade key, and how much of the bonded yield is held by a single protocol.

**Until then, treat Blast as unscored on purpose** — not "failed", just not
yet measured by the terminal's methodology.
