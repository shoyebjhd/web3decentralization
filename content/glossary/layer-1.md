Layer 1 (L1) is the base blockchain that settles transactions and secures the
network itself — Bitcoin, Ethereum, Solana, Cardano. Everything else either
lives on an L1 or routes through one for final settlement.

## Why it matters

L1s are the "do it yourself" blockchains, and their decentralization defines
the security of everything built on top. Comparing L1s — validators, stake,
clients, governance — is exactly what the W3D terminal does, because the L1
is the trust root of the whole stack.
