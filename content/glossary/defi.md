DeFi — decentralized finance — is financial software that runs on blockchains
instead of banks: lending, borrowing, trading, staking, and savings, executed by
smart contracts. No bank account, no permission, no closing time — the code is
the banker.

## Why it matters

DeFi is the biggest real use of "code instead of trust." Anyone anywhere can
borrow or earn without an application, but "no permission" also means "no
protection": a hacked or malicious contract takes your money with no appeals
desk. Understanding what the code actually does is the job.
