The base fee is the mandatory, protocol-set portion of every Ethereum
transaction fee (since [EIP-1559](glossary/eip-1559.md)): it rises when blocks
are full and falls when they're empty — and it gets *burned*, permanently
removing ETH from supply.

## Why it matters

Burning the base fee turned ETH potentially deflationary during busy periods
— "ultrasound money" — directly linking network usage to holder value. For
users, it means fee estimates are saner than the old blind auctions; for
analysts, the burn rate is a live gauge of real economic demand for block
space.
