Proof of reserves is an exchange publishing cryptographic evidence it holds
customer funds — typically a Merkle tree of balances plus wallet signatures
proving control. Post-FTX, it became table stakes for centralized platforms
claiming solvency.

## Why it matters

Reserves without liabilities prove nothing — an exchange can hold $1B and owe
$2B (FTX's lesson), which is why proof-of-reserves *plus* proof-of-liabilities
is the actual standard to demand. Treat solo reserve attestations as a good
start and an incomplete answer; the complete answer is still "withdraw to
self-custody."
