A private key is the secret number that authorizes transactions from a wallet.
Anyone who holds it can spend the funds it controls. Your wallet derives your
public address from the key — the address is visible to everyone, but only the
key can move the coins.

## Why it matters

There is no password reset on a public blockchain. Lose your private key and
the coins are gone forever; share it and anyone can drain your wallet. Smarter
than memorizing a raw key: your [seed phrase](seed-phrase.md) recreates all
your keys. The most important security habit in crypto is keeping keys private.
