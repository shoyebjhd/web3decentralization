Web3 is the umbrella idea of a next internet where ownership, identity, and
logic move from big companies to blockchains you can verify — your wallet
signs, your data is yours, and "the platform" is code, not a corporation.

## Why it matters

The name is aspirational, and the gap between marketing and reality is wide —
many "Web3" products keep the sensitive parts centralized. That's exactly the
gap W3D was built to measure: separating genuine decentralization from a
token-gated facade.
