BNB Chain is Binance's EVM-compatible L1: fast, cheap, enormous ecosystem —
and validator-capped (a few dozen elected validators, heavily
exchange-adjacent stake). The trade is explicit: performance and low fees for
concentrated control.

## How it works

Proof-of-staked-authority with 21-ish active validators producing 3-second
blocks. Low fees attract massive retail activity (DEXs, games, memecoins);
Binance's ecosystem (exchange, wallet, launchpad) funnels users on-chain.

## Why it matters for decentralization

BNB Chain is the honest counterexample analysts need: wildly successful by
usage, weak by every decentralization pillar — few validators, exchange-held
stake, corporate upgrade control. It proves demand doesn't require
decentralization, which is exactly why scoring must stay independent of
popularity.

## Risks & trade-offs

Validator concentration + exchange dependence (outages and freezes have
happened); regulatory exposure via Binance; and bridge/cross-chain trust
assumptions inherited from its architecture.

## FAQ

**Is BNB Chain "really" decentralized?** By W3D-style pillars, no — and it
doesn't pretend otherwise. Judge it as fast corporate infrastructure, not a
censorship-resistant network.

**Why do people use it then?** Fees under a cent and everything works. The
market has repeatedly chosen cheap over decentralized.

**BNB the token?** Gas + fee discounts + launchpad access — exchange-token
economics, not a decentralization story.
