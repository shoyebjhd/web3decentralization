Circulating supply is the number of tokens currently tradable on the open
market — excluding locked team tokens, unvested allocations, and unreleased
treasury. [Market cap](market-cap.md) uses this number; fully-diluted value
uses the total that will ever exist.

## Why it matters

Low circulating supply with a huge locked overhang is the classic setup for
"high FDV, low float" dumps: price looks supported until
[unlocks](token-unlock.md) flood the market. Always compare circulating vs
total supply — the gap is future selling waiting for a date.
