A custodial wallet is one where someone else holds the
[keys](glossary/private-key.md) — an exchange account, a fintech app, an ETF.
You log in with a password; they control the blockchain. Convenient and
recoverable, exactly like a bank account, with the same counterparty risks.

## Why it matters

Custodial is how most people *start* (and how billions were lost at Mt. Gox,
FTX, and others): the custodian can freeze, lend, lose, or be ordered to
seize your funds. Use custodians as on-ramps, not vaults — buy there,
withdraw to [self-custody](glossary/self-custody.md), and size what you leave
behind by what you'd survive losing.
