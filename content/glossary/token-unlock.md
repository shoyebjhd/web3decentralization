A token unlock is a scheduled release of previously locked tokens into
circulation — team [vesting](vesting.md) cliffs, investor tranches, ecosystem
funds. Unlock calendars are public, and big unlocks routinely pressure prices
as recipients sell.

## Why it matters

Unlocks are the most predictable price events in crypto — yet beginners buy
right into them. A token with 20% circulating and monthly unlocks for two
years has a very different investment case than its current
[market cap](market-cap.md) suggests. Check the unlock schedule the way stock
investors check earnings dates.
