A testnet is a parallel blockchain that works almost exactly like the real one
— but the coins are worthless test tokens handed out for free. Developers deploy
and stress-test smart contracts there before risking real funds on mainnet.

## Why it matters

Testnets let beginners practice safely: you can break things, drain a "wallet,"
and send transactions that cost nothing without real consequences. They're also
why you should never buy "testnet coins" from scammers — they're free by
design.
