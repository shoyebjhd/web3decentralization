title: "Difficulty Adjustment"
related: [mining, proof-of-work, block-reward]
---

The difficulty adjustment retargets Bitcoin's mining puzzle every 2,016 blocks
(~two weeks) so blocks keep arriving every ~10 minutes no matter how much
hash power joins or leaves. More miners → harder puzzles; exodus → easier
ones, automatically.

## Why it matters

This quiet mechanism is Bitcoin's survival superpower: after China's 2021
mining ban wiped out half the hash rate overnight, blocks slowed for weeks —
then difficulty fell, mining became profitable elsewhere, and the network
healed itself with no committee vote. Self-tuning security no boardroom could
replicate.
