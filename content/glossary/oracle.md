An oracle brings off-chain data onto the blockchain. Smart contracts can't
browse the web, so an oracle feeds them signed facts — a token's price, a
flight's status, a game's score — that the contract can then use as truth.

## Why it matters

"Garbage in, garbage out" is existential for DeFi: if a price oracle is wrong or
manipulated, loans get liquidated unfairly and positions stolen. A single
centralized oracle is a single point of failure, which is why chains of
independent oracles (like Chainlink) matter for decentralization.
