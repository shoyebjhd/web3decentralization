---
title: "Validity Proof"
related: [zk-rollup, zero-knowledge-proof, fraud-proof]
---

A validity proof (usually a ZK proof) demonstrates a computation was correct
*before* anyone needs to trust it — no challenge window, no watchers
required. Post the proof, verify it cheaply on-chain, finalize in minutes.
It's the "innocent, proven" counterpart to fraud proofs' "guilty until
challenged."

## Why it matters

Validity proofs are why ZK rollups settle fast and inherit security cleanly —
but generating them is computationally brutal, which is why proving is
centralizing into specialized hardware operators today. Watch the prover
market the way you watch validators: whoever proves at scale holds a new kind
of power.
