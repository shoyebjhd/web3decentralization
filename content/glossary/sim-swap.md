---
title: "SIM Swap"
related: [phishing, exchange, wallet]
---

A SIM swap is when an attacker convinces your phone carrier to move your
number to their SIM — intercepting every SMS code meant for you, including
exchange 2FA and password resets. With your number, they reset your email,
then your exchange, then withdraw. No malware needed: just a call center and
your leaked personal details.

## Why it matters

SMS 2FA is barely better than nothing against a targeted attacker, and phone
numbers are the skeleton key to most people's digital lives. Defenses, in
order: authenticator apps or hardware keys instead of SMS everywhere;
carrier PIN/port-freeze; exchange withdrawal allowlists; and keeping only
spending money anywhere SMS-recoverable.
