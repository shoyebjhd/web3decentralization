A token is a digital asset issued on an existing blockchain — often Ethereum
or Solana — as a smart contract. Unlike a *coin* (which powers its own chain),
a token rides on top of someone else's network. Tokens can be utility,
governance, stable, or security-like assets; ERC-20 is the most famous
standard.

## Why it matters

Tokens are how projects distribute ownership, rewards, and governance without
building their own blockchain. Their supply and distribution rules decide a
project's market — and how decentralized its ownership truly is — which is
exactly what W3D's capital scores measure.
