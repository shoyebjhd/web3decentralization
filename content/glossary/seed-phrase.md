A seed phrase (also "recovery phrase") is a list of 12–24 words that is the
master key to a wallet. From it, every private key in that wallet can be
mathematically recreated — so you never need to back up each key, just the
words, in exact order.

## Why it matters

The seed phrase is the one thing you must protect and the one thing you must
not lose. Write it on paper, store it offline, never type it into websites or
an app you don't trust. Anyone with your seed phrase owns everything in that
wallet — sharing it hands them your funds.
