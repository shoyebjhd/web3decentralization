Phishing is the most common way crypto gets stolen without any blockchain hack:
a fake website, email, or DM that looks like the real service, tricking you
into entering your seed phrase or approving a malicious contract. The attacker
then drains your wallet.

## Why it matters

In crypto, one mistake = permanent loss — no bank dispute, no undo. The
anti-phishing habits matter more than any technology: check the exact domain,
never type your seed phrase anywhere, never connect your wallet to a site you
didn't verify, and move slowly when money moves.
