Slippage is the difference between the price you expect and the price you
actually get on a trade. It happens when your order moves the market —
especially in thin liquidity pools. DEXs let you set a "slippage tolerance":
approve up to 1‑3% worse than quoted, or the trade fails.

## Why it matters

Setting slippage too high lets sandwich bots front-run you for the maximum
difference; too low and valid trades fail repeatedly. Understanding slippage is
a basic DEX survival skill — and a big trade in a thin pool is a
[rug pull](rug-pull.md) risk signal.
