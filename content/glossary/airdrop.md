An airdrop distributes free tokens to eligible wallets — usually to reward early
users, seed a network's owners, or generate buzz before a launch. Airdrops are
how a new token spreads ownership, with eligibility often based on past
activity or a snapshot date.

## Why it matters

Airdrops are a distribution-quality signal: a token widely scattered in small
amounts starts more decentralized than one handed to insiders. They're also a
phishing playground — scammers fake airdrops, ask you to "connect your wallet
to claim," and drain it. Real airdrops never ask you to connect or pay.
