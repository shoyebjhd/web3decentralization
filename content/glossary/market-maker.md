---
title: "Market Maker"
related: [order-book, liquidity, exchange]
---

A market maker quotes both buy and sell prices continuously, profiting from
the spread while keeping markets liquid. In crypto they range from regulated
trading firms to anonymous wallets to the protocols themselves (many "DeFi"
yields are just paid market-making).

## Why it matters

Someone must always take the other side of your trade — understanding who,
and what they're paid, explains spreads, fake volume (wash trading to feign
liquidity), and why new tokens offer "market-making deals" that are really
disguised dumps. Healthy markets have competing makers; dead ones have one
bot and a prayer.
