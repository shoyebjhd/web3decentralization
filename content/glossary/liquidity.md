Liquidity is how easily an asset can be bought or sold without moving its
price. More liquidity = tighter prices and smaller spreads; thin liquidity = a
single big order swings the market. On DEXs, it's provided by people who
deposit token pairs into pools and earn fees.

## Why it matters

Liquidity is the friction that decides whether decentralized markets work. It's
also a decentralization signal: a token whose supply is mostly in a few
unlocked pools on one exchange is fragile; broad, deep, widely distributed
liquidity is stronger.
