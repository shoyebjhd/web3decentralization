Market cap is a token's total circulating supply × its current price. It's the
usual "how big is this crypto" number, used to rank coins and judge headroom —
a 30× on a $500M token is a different risk than a 30× on a $200B one.

## Why it matters

Market cap is frequently misused as "total money invested" — it isn't; it's a
mark-to-market snapshot of the last trade. Fully-diluted value (supply planned)
matters too. It's a useful headline, never the story by itself.
