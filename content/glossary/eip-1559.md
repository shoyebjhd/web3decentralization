EIP-1559 (August 2021) rewrote Ethereum's fee market: instead of blind
first-price auctions, each block has an algorithmic [base fee](glossary/base-fee.md)
that gets burned, plus an optional tip. Fees became predictable; ETH gained a
burn mechanism.

## Why it matters

It's the rare upgrade that improved UX *and* monetary policy at once —
wallets can estimate fees reliably, and heavy usage now shrinks supply. It
didn't lower fees on average (a persistent myth — only scaling does that),
but it made them legible, which for users and analysts matters almost as
much.
