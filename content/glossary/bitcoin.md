Bitcoin is the first cryptocurrency — a peer-to-peer digital money launched in
2009 by the pseudonymous Satoshi Nakamoto. No company issues it and no bank
records it; the rules live in code and the ledger is maintained by a global
network of volunteers.

## Why it matters

Bitcoin invented the blockchain and is still the reference point for
decentralization. It's capped at 21 million coins, secured by energy-intensive
proof-of-work mining, and runs with no single point of control — which is why
it tops W3D's decentralization scores.
