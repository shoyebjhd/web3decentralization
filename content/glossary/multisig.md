Multisig — "multi-signature" — means a wallet or transaction requires more
than one private key to authorize. A typical setup is 2-of-3: three key
holders, any two must sign before funds move or a transaction executes.

## Why it matters

Multisig removes the single point of failure. A project treasury, a DAO, or an
individual can structure it so no one person can steal or freeze funds and no
single lost key locks everyone out. It's the standard way serious protocols
secure their treasuries.
