A stablecoin is a cryptocurrency designed to hold a constant value — almost
always $1.00. It's a token on a blockchain such as USDT, USDC, or DAI that you
can spend, trade, lend, or save without accepting Bitcoin's price swings. It
brings stable money onto a blockchain.

## Why it matters

Stablecoins are the "cash" of crypto: a way to hold value in dollars while
still operating inside the permissionless world of blockchains. They power
most crypto trading, DeFi lending, and cross-border transfers.
