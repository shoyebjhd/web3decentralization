APY — annual percentage yield — is the yearly return on an investment assuming
rewards are reinvested and compounded. A 20% APY means a deposit roughly grows
20% per year if the rate stays steady and you keep compounding.

## Why it matters

APY is the headline number of every staking/lending pitch, but it's a
projection, not a promise: rates move, prices move, and "APY" is often quoted
in a token that's declining. High APY is a *warning light*, not just an
opportunity — especially when it's denominated in new minted tokens.
