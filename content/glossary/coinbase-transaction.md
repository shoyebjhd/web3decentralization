The coinbase transaction is each block's first entry: it creates new coins
from nothing and pays them (plus fees) to the winning miner. Not to be
confused with the Coinbase company — this one predates it and is how every
Bitcoin in existence entered circulation.

## Why it matters

Coinbase transactions are the only "minting" in Bitcoin — the complete,
auditable record of all 21M coins' birth certificates. Analysts trace them to
study miner behavior, selling pressure, and the slow migration of supply from
miners to holders over each epoch.
