An IDO (Initial DEX Offering) launches a token directly on a decentralized
exchange — liquidity pool created, trading open to anyone instantly, no
gatekeepers. Fairer access than ICO-era whitelists, but the same insider
dynamics underneath.

## Why it matters

IDOs democratized *access* without democratizing *information*: teams and
early backers still position beforehand, bots snipe the opening blocks, and
retail still buys the top of the launch candle. Treat every IDO as a professional
rigged casino where you're welcome to play — with size discipline and instant
skepticism toward day-one charts.
