Staking is the Proof-of-Stake version of mining: you lock up ("stake") tokens
as collateral to help secure a network and earn rewards. Anyone can run a
validator if they meet the stake requirement, or delegate their tokens to an
existing validator. Bad behavior can be punished by "slashing" — losing part of
the stake.

## Why it matters

How stake is distributed is a central part of a network's decentralization.
If a few giant pools or exchanges control most of the stake, a handful of
decision-makers effectively run the network. W3D's capital pillar scores
exactly this concentration.
