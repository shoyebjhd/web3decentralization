An exchange is a platform where you buy, sell, and trade crypto. There are two
kinds: **centralized** (CEX) — a company that keeps an order book, holds your
funds, and handles fiat — and **decentralized** (DEX) — smart contracts on a
blockchain where you trade directly from your wallet.

## Why it matters

Centralized exchanges are the main on-ramp from money to crypto (and where KYC
applies), but they hold your coins and have been hacked. Decentralized
exchanges offer permissionless trading with no custody — usually at the cost
of liquidity and ease of fiat. Which one you use is largely a security and
privacy decision.
