The all-time high (ATH) is the highest price an asset has ever printed.
Breaking it triggers euphoria, headlines, and [FOMO](glossary/fomo.md)
inflows; assets sitting 80% below theirs define [bear markets](glossary/bear-market.md).
"Distance from ATH" is crypto's quickest sentiment thermometer.

## Why it matters

ATHs are where supply meets psychology: previous buyers finally break even
and sell (resistance), while breakout buyers pile in (momentum). Buying
exactly at euphoric ATHs is the classic beginner mistake — not because ATHs
can't go higher, but because risk/reward there demands a plan, not excitement.
