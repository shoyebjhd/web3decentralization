Proof of Stake (PoS) is a consensus mechanism where users lock up ("stake")
tokens as collateral to secure a network, replacing the energy-hungry
competitive mining of Proof of Work. The protocol picks validators to propose
and confirm blocks in proportion to their stake, and can "slash" stake that
misbehaves.

## Why it matters

PoS shifts who secures the network from machine-power to money-at-risk, which
is far cheaper to run. But it raises a decentralization question: if a few big
pools or exchanges control most of the stake, power quietly centralizes even in
a PoS system.
