Vesting is a lockup schedule that releases tokens gradually — team and
investor allocations typically vest over 1–4 years instead of dumping on day
one. It's meant to align insiders with long-term holders.

## Why it matters

Vesting schedules are future sell pressure with a date attached. A project can
look fairly distributed today while 60% of supply unlocks for insiders over
the next two years. Always read the [unlock](token-unlock.md) calendar before
buying: you're pricing in not just today's supply, but tomorrow's.
