The Howey Test is the US Supreme Court's four-part test for "is this an
investment contract (a security)?": money invested, in a common enterprise,
with profit expectations, derived from others' efforts. Fail any prong and
it's (probably) not a security; meet all four and the SEC claims jurisdiction.

## Why it matters

Nearly every token sale trips at least three prongs, which is why US
exchanges delist aggressively and projects geoblock Americans — regulatory
risk shapes which assets you can even access. You don't need a law degree,
just the reflex: centralized team + promised profits + your money = expect
regulators eventually, and price that risk.
