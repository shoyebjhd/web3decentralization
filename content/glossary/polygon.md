# Polygon

Polygon is a multi-chain Ethereum ecosystem — a single "Polygon" brand
covering the PoS chain, zkEVM, AggLayer, and a growing set of L2s (with
chain-specific entries in this glossary under `polygon-zkevm`). On the W3D
Terminal the plain Polygon network's decentralization score is **still
pending** — it is audited under the zkEVM slug (`polygon-zkevm`) for the
rollup-scale software/sequencer pillars, and its composite `/100` has not
been published for every Polygon surface.

**Why the wording matters:** "Polygon" is a family, not one chain. A score
for `polygon` without specifying *which* Polygon — PoS chain, zkEVM rollup,
or an AggLayer-connected chain — is the kind of measure that misleads.
That's exactly why the terminal separates the slugs and only scores what it
can actually measure.

**What to check instead of a single number:** sequencer/aggregator control
(does one team operate it?), the upgrade key holder, and validator-set
spread on whichever specific Polygon you care about.
