A smart contract is a program stored on a blockchain that runs exactly as
written when its conditions are met. Once deployed, nobody can change it —
not the creator, not the exchange, not anyone. The "contract" is the code
itself, and the execution is automated and verifiable.

## Why it matters

Smart contracts let you do business with code instead of trust: a loan that
executes itself, a token that can't be double-spent, an exchange with no
employees. They're the building block of DeFi, DAOs, and most of Web3 — and
their immutability is why a bug in the code is a bug forever.
