A fiat-backed stablecoin (USDC, USDT) promises 1 token = $1 held in reserve —
cash, T-bills, and equivalents at banks and custodians, attested periodically.
Redeem with the issuer (after [KYC](glossary/kyc.md)) and burn the token;
anyone can mint by depositing dollars.

## Why it matters

They're the safest stable design in practice and the most centralized in
principle: issuers can freeze any address, block whole countries, and must be
trusted about reserves (Tether's history of vague attestations is the
cautionary tale). A fiat stablecoin is a bank deposit with blockchain
rails — audited trust, not trustlessness. Diversify issuers accordingly.
