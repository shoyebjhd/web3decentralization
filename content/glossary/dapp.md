A dApp — decentralized application — is an app whose logic runs on a blockchain
via smart contracts, not on a private server. The front end you see is just a
website; the important rules live in publicly verifiable code no single company
controls.

## Why it matters

"Backend on the blockchain" means no kill switch, no exchange to trust, and no
central database to breach. But it also comes with real UX friction (wallets,
gas fees, approval prompts). Learning to judge whether a dApp is *actually*
decentralized or just wrapping central servers is a core Web3 skill.
