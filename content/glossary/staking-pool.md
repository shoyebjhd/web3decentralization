A staking pool combines many holders' tokens to run validators collectively —
splitting costs, minimums, and rewards proportionally. Exchange pools, liquid
staking protocols, and community pools are the three flavors; all solve the
same problem: most holders can't (or won't) run infrastructure.

## Why it matters

Pools are decentralization's double edge: they let small holders participate
and earn — while concentrating the network's actual validating power in a few
giant operators. Lido-scale pools controlling a third of staked ETH are the
textbook case. Pool with awareness: spread across operators, prefer smaller
independent ones, and watch concentration metrics, not just APY.
