Consensus is the ruleset by which independent nodes agree on the one true
history of the ledger. It's what decides who gets to add the next block and
what happens when two nodes see conflicting chains. Proof-of-work and
proof-of-stake are two different consensus mechanisms.

## Why it matters

Without consensus, thousands of computers with no leader couldn't agree on
anything — you'd get parallel realities. Consensus replaces the trusted third
party with a protocol any node can verify. The decentralization of that
process is precisely what W3D's infrastructure scores measure.
