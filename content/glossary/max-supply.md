Max supply is the hard cap on how many tokens will ever exist — 21 million
for [Bitcoin](bitcoin.md), none at all for inflationary tokens like ETH or
SOL. It's set in code (or by governance) and defines the asset's scarcity
story.

## Why it matters

"Capped supply" is crypto's core scarcity pitch, but the number alone means
little without the *emission schedule*: 21M arriving over 140 years behaves
nothing like 1B unlocking in two. Scarcity is a schedule, not a slogan — read
both numbers.
