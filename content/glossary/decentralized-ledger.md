title: "Decentralized Ledger"
related: [blockchain, node, decentralization]
---

A decentralized ledger is a record of transactions that no single party owns.
Instead of one bank or company keeping the official copy, a network of
independent computers each hold the same history and agree on updates through
consensus. A blockchain is the most common type.

## Why it matters

When the record is shared across many independent parties, any single actor —
or small group — can't quietly rewrite history or shut the system down. That
distributed ownership of the record is what makes the system decentralized in
the first place.
