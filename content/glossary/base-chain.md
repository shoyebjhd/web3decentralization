Base is Coinbase's Ethereum L2, built on the [OP Stack](glossary/op-stack.md):
cheap, EVM-equivalent transactions with Coinbase's distribution behind it —
fiat on-ramps, 100M+ verified users one click from on-chain. The biggest
corporate bet on the [Superchain](glossary/superchain.md) vision.

## How it works

Standard optimistic-rollup mechanics (batches to Ethereum, fraud-proof
security, 7-day withdrawals) plus Coinbase-operated sequencer and deep
exchange integration. No native token — gas paid in ETH.

## Why it matters for decentralization

Base is the centralization stress test made flesh: massive adoption through
a trusted corporation, running infrastructure only Coinbase controls today,
with credible commitments to decentralize (fault proofs, open sequencing).
Watch whether distribution converts to decentralization — or just extends
Coinbase's moat on-chain.

## Risks & trade-offs

Single corporate sequencer and upgrade authority; regulatory exposure via the
parent company; no-token means no community governance counterweight (yet);
and Superchain dependence for the interoperability story.

## FAQ

**Is Base decentralized?** Not today — openly Stage 1 with a corporate
operator. The question is trajectory, and the milestones are public: fault
proofs live, decentralized sequencing pending.

**Why no BASE token?** Deliberate: avoids securities questions and keeps the
chain credibly neutral infrastructure. Governance arrives via the Collective.

**Should beginners start here?** For cheap practice with training wheels,
yes — with eyes open that training wheels are centralized.
