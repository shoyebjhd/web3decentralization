title: "Social Recovery"

---

Social recovery replaces the lone [seed phrase](glossary/seed-phrase.md) with
a circle of trusted people or devices: lose access, and a threshold of your
"guardians" (friends, family, a second device) together authorize a new key.
No single guardian can steal anything alone.

## Why it matters

Seed phrases fail ordinary humans — lost paper ends ownership permanently.
Social recovery trades cryptographic purity for human reality: keys you can
actually recover, at the cost of trusting your circle not to collude. Done
with widely separated, non-communicating guardians, it's the most promising
path to mass [self-custody](glossary/self-custody.md) that doesn't end in
tears.
