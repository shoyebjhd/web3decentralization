Delegation is when a token holder assigns their stake to a validator instead of
running one. The validator proposes and confirms blocks; you and they share the
rewards (minus a fee), and if the validator misbehaves you can be slashed —
so choosing a good validator matters.

## Why it matters

Delegation is how small holders participate in PoS security, which sounds
democratic — but it pools power: the biggest delegation services attract the
most stake, recreating centralization. Distribution across validators is one of
the clearest decentralization indicators there is.
