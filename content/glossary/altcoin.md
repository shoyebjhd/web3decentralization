An altcoin is any cryptocurrency that isn't Bitcoin. The term covers Ethereum
and all other layer-1 chains, tokens, and even joke coins — everything from
serious networks to outright scams.

## Why it matters

"Altcoin" bundles a wildly diverse set: legitimate protocols with real
products, speculative tokens, and rug-pull traps. Treating everything outside
Bitcoin as equivalent is a rookie mistake — the difference between a real
chain and a pump-and-dump is exactly what decentralized analysis is meant to
expose.
