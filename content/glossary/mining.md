Mining is the PoW process of running hardware at high speed, guessing hashes
repeatedly, until a guess meets the network's difficulty target. The first
miner to succeed proposes the next block and is paid in newly minted coins plus
fees. "Miner" is what you call a node doing this work.

## Why it matters

Mining decentralizes who controls the history: anyone with the right hardware
anywhere can compete, and no one grants permission. But it concentrates cost &
hardware (and, on good networks, nothing else). Distribution of that hash
power is a pillar of real decentralization.
