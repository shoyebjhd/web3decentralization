title: "Utility Token"
related: [token, tokenomics, gas]
---

A utility token unlocks something inside a project — paying fees, accessing
features, getting discounts — rather than representing ownership. BNB's fee
discounts and in-game currencies are classic examples.

## Why it matters

"Utility" is the most abused word in token pitches: if the token's only use
is paying for something that could be priced in dollars, demand is artificial
and the price usually trends toward zero. Real utility tokens capture value
because the service is genuinely cheaper or better *with* the token.
