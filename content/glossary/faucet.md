title: "Faucet"
related: [testnet, transaction, wallet]

A faucet drips free testnet tokens so developers (and learners) can practice
without spending real money — connect a wallet, solve a captcha, receive
play ETH. Every major testnet runs official faucets, plus community ones when
those rate-limit.

## Why it matters

Faucets are the on-ramp to safe practice: every lesson urging "try on
testnet first" assumes you can get test tokens in a minute. Never pay for
testnet coins (scammers sell worthless faucet drips), and never confuse
testnet and mainnet addresses when real funds are nearby.
