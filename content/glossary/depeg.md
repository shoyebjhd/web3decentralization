A depeg is when a stablecoin breaks its $1.00 peg — trading at $0.97, $0.60,
or $0.0001. Minor wobbles happen in volatility; a sustained depeg means the
market no longer believes each token is redeemable for a dollar.

## Why it matters

"Stable" is a promise, not a property. UST's depeg vaporized tens of billions
because the entire Terra ecosystem assumed the peg was a law of nature.
Before holding any stablecoin at size, ask: what backs it, who audits the
backing, and what happens on the worst day?
