A veToken (vote-escrowed token, pioneered by Curve's veCRV) is what you get
for locking a [governance token](glossary/governance-token.md) for months or
years: more voting power and boosted rewards the longer you lock. Exit early
and you forfeit the boost — commitment literally priced in.

## Why it matters

Vote-escrow aligns voters with the protocol's future instead of mercenary
farming — and spawned "bribe markets" where protocols pay ve-holders for
votes directing emissions their way. Clever mechanism design, but also proof
that governance power, once tokenized, gets bought: follow who accumulates
the ve-supply and you've found the real bosses.
