Taproot (activated November 2021) is Bitcoin's biggest upgrade in years:
smarter signatures (Schnorr) that make complex spending conditions look like
ordinary single-key payments — cheaper, more private, and enabling discreet
[smart-contract](glossary/smart-contract.md)-like logic on Bitcoin.

## Why it matters

Taproot quietly expanded what Bitcoin can do without changing what Bitcoin
*is*: cooperative multisig and Lightning channels now blend into normal
traffic, improving both privacy and efficiency. It also enabled Ordinals and
new scripting experiments — proof that even the most conservative chain can
evolve, slowly and carefully.
