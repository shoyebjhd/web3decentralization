A 51% attack is when one actor controls more than half of a network's mining
power (or staked value), letting them rewrite history — double-spend coins,
reorder or censor transactions. It's the theoretical ceiling on how decentralized
a network must be.

## Why it matters

The Nakamoto Coefficient is literally counting "how many actors could you team
up to reach 51%?" High hash difficulty on a huge network like Bitcoin makes it
impossible; small chains with thin hash power get 51%-attacked regularly. This
is the single clearest exposure of a network's real decentralization.
