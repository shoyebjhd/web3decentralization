A DEX aggregator (like 1inch or Jupiter) splits your trade across many DEXs
and pools to find the best overall price — instead of you manually checking
five exchanges. One transaction, routed optimally.

## Why it matters

Aggregation usually beats any single DEX on price and
[slippage](slippage.md), especially for large trades. The catch is the same
as all DeFi routing: you're trusting the aggregator's smart contracts *in
addition to* every pool it touches. More hops, more code, more risk surface.
