Self-custody means you alone hold the private keys to your crypto, with no
bank or exchange in the middle. The counterpart is custodial storage, where an
exchange or third party holds your keys — and your coins — for you.

## Why it matters

Self-custody removes third-party risk (the exchange can't freeze, lose, or
misuse your funds), but moves responsibility entirely to you: lose the keys
and the coins are gone forever. "Not your keys, not your coins" is the phrase
that sums up both the freedom and the burden.
