Perpetual futures ("perps") are leveraged bets on a token's price with no
expiry date — you post margin, pick long or short, and a funding rate keeps
the price tracking spot. dYdX and Hyperliquid brought them fully on-chain.

## Why it matters

Perps are where most crypto leverage lives, which makes them where most
retail money *dies*: high leverage + [volatility](volatility.md) +
auto-[liquidation](liquidation.md) is a designed wealth transfer from the
impatient to the patient. Understand funding rates and liquidation prices
before touching leverage — or better, don't.
