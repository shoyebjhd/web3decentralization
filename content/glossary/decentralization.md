Decentralization means **no single party controls a system**. In blockchains it
isn't a yes/no switch — it's a spectrum across several dimensions: who runs the
infrastructure, how ownership and stake are distributed, who can change the
rules, and how diverse the software is.

## Why it matters

A network that is only "decentralized" in name can still be controlled by a few
operators, a few big holders, or a single client. W3D's terminal scores chains
across four pillars — infrastructure, capital, governance, and software — so
you can see where a network is genuinely open and where it still concentrates
power.
