A crypto wallet is a tool that stores the keys that control your coins. It
"holds" nothing physically — it keeps your private keys and lets you sign
transactions, and it shows your balances by reading the blockchain. Wallets
come as apps, browser extensions, hardware devices, or paper.

## Why it matters

Self-custody — holding your own keys — is the whole point of "[not your keys,
not your coins](https://web3decentralization.com/what-is-a-seed-phrase/)".
Whoever has your private keys controls your funds; a wallet is how you keep
that power safely in your own hands.
