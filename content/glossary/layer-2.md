Layer 2 (L2) is a system built *on top* of a Layer 1 that processes
transactions faster and cheaper, then settles the results back to the L1. The
main flavors are rollups — optimistic and zero-knowledge — which batch many
transactions into one L1 submission.

## Why it matters

L2s are how blockchains scale without sacrificing the L1's security. The L2
inherit's "here are the facts", but who runs the L2's sequencer and how exits
and disputes work decide its *own* decentralization — quite separate from the
L1's.
