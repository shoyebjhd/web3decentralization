A governance token gives holders voting rights over a protocol — fee levels,
treasury spending, upgrades. UNI, ARB, and MKR are famous examples. One token
usually equals one vote, so voting power concentrates with the biggest
holders.

## Why it matters

Governance tokens are how DeFi distributes ownership — and how it often
*fakes* decentralization: a DAO where three wallets hold 60% of votes is a
company with extra steps. Before trusting any "community-governed" claim,
check the actual vote distribution, not the forum vibes.
