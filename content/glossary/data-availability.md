---
title: "Data Availability"
related: [rollup, layer-2, blockchain]
---

Data availability is the guarantee that a rollup's transaction data is
actually published somewhere anyone can check — not just a claim that "trust
us, it happened." Without it, nobody can build fraud proofs or reconstruct
state, and the rollup's security collapses to trusting the operator.

## Why it matters

"Where's the data?" separates real rollups (data on Ethereum) from pretenders
(data on a committee server). Data-availability layers like Celestia and
Ethereum's blobs exist to make publication cheap — because verification
without data is theater.
