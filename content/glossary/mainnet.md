The mainnet is the live, production blockchain where real transactions happen
and real tokens have value. Launching on mainnet is the moment a protocol "goes
live" — no test money, real assets, real users, real risk.

## Why it matters

For users, "is this on mainnet?" separates real from vaporware-backed hype.
Developers test first on a testnet to avoid expensive mistakes, then migrate.
Most scam or half-finished projects never reach a true mainnet, so it's a quick
reality check.
