Gwei is the everyday unit for Ethereum [gas](gas.md) prices: 1 gwei = 0.000000001
ETH (a billionth). When wallets quote "25 gwei," that's the per-unit price of
computation — a simple transfer at 21,000 gas costs 21,000 × 25 gwei.

## Why it matters

Watching gwei is watching Ethereum's congestion in real time: 5 gwei means a
quiet chain, 200+ means an NFT mint or market panic is pricing everyone out.
It's the number that decides whether your transaction costs cents or tens of
dollars — and whether you should wait an hour.
