A fork is a split in a blockchain's history or its rules. A **software fork**
upgrades the code; a **chain fork** happens when nodes run incompatible rules
and the history splits in two. A hard fork creates an incompatible break (Bitcoin
→ Bitcoin Cash); a soft fork is backward-compatible.

## Why it matters

Forks are blockchain governance in action — who gets to change the rules when
there's no CEO? Bitcoin's forks produced rival coins; Ethereum's fork after
the DAO hack rewrote history. How a community resolves "what the rules are" is
a direct test of whether its decentralization is real or theatrical.
