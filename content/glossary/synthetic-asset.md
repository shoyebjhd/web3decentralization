A synthetic asset tracks another asset's price without holding it — on-chain
tokens mirroring stocks, commodities, or currencies via
[collateral](glossary/collateral.md) plus [oracle](glossary/oracle.md) prices.
Synthetix pioneered them: lock SNX, mint sUSD/sAAPL/sTSLA, trade 24/7 with no
broker.

## Why it matters

Synthetics deliver global access to anything priceable — and concentrate
exactly the risks beginners underestimate: oracle trust, collateral
reflexivity, and liquidity that vanishes in volatility. When the peg
mechanism is "incentives plus hope," size positions like the experiment it
is.
