title: "Permissionless"

Permissionless means anyone can participate with no approval — run a node,
send a transaction, deploy a contract, build an app. No signup, no license,
no gatekeeper. Bitcoin and Ethereum are permissionless at the protocol level;
an exchange account is the opposite.

## Why it matters

Permissionlessness is the property everything else defends: censorship
resistance, open innovation, and exit rights all flow from "nobody can stop
you." When a chain adds allowlists, licensed validators, or CEX-only access,
it's trading away the core thing — measure what remains.
