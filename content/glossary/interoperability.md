Interoperability is blockchains talking to each other — moving assets, data,
and calls across networks via [bridges](bridge.md), shared protocols (IBC on
Cosmos), or multi-chain designs (Polkadot). The vision: many specialized
chains acting like one system.

## Why it matters

No single chain will do everything, so the seams between chains are where the
ecosystem's value — and risk — concentrates. Every interoperability mechanism
is a trust assumption to audit: IBC light clients are strong,
multisig-guarded bridges much less so. The future is multi-chain; the present
is multi-risk.
