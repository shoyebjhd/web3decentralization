Solo staking means running your own validator with your own stake — your
machine, your keys, your responsibility — instead of delegating to a pool or
a liquid-staking provider. On Ethereum it requires 32 ETH and a reliably
online computer.

## Why it matters

Solo stakers are decentralization's front line: every independent validator
dilutes the power of giant pools. The ecosystem actively subsidizes them
(better tooling, lower hardware needs) precisely because stake centralizing
into two or three providers would quietly un-decentralize the network they
secure.
