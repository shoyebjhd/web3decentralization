Total Value Locked (TVL) is the dollar value of all assets deposited in a
DeFi protocol — its pools, vaults, and lending markets combined. It's the
standard headline metric for "how big is this protocol."

## Why it matters

TVL measures trust and usage, but it's gameable: protocols bribe deposits with
inflated token rewards (which evaporate), and one whale can be half the TVL.
Treat TVL as a starting signal, then check *who* deposited, *what* tokens,
and whether the yield paying them is sustainable.
