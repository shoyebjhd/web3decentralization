title: "Yield Farming"
related: [defi, liquidity, apy]
---

Yield farming is the practice of moving your tokens between DeFi protocols to
maximize returns — providing liquidity, lending, staking — often chasing the
protocol's freshly minted governance-token rewards on top of trading fees.

## Why it matters

Farming created DeFi's boom '*and* its danger: yields of thousands of percent
were real, but they were usually paid in tokens that were themselves
speculative. In healthy form it's "earning for providing a real service";
in hype form it's a liquidity extraction machine with impermanent-loss and
hack risk attached.
