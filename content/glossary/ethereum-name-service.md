The Ethereum Name Service (ENS) maps human names like `vitalik.eth` to
machine [addresses](address.md) — crypto's version of DNS. Register a name as
an NFT, point it at your wallets, and receive payments at something you can
actually read out loud.

## Why it matters

ENS kills the single scariest beginner moment — sending to a 42-character hex
string you can't verify. It's also real decentralized infrastructure: names
are NFTs you own, not rented usernames a company can seize. Always still
double-check the resolved address before large transfers.
