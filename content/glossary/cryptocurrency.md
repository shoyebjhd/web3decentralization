Cryptocurrency is digital money that lives on a blockchain and is transferred
without a bank or other central issuer. "Crypto" — Bitcoin being the first and
best known — refers to the cryptography used to secure the ledger and prove
ownership.

## Why it matters

Because no central party issues it or records who owns what, cryptocurrency
offers something regular money can't: ownership that no bank can freeze and
transfers that no middleman can block. It's also the fuel and use-case that
made the underlying blockchain technology real and valuable.
