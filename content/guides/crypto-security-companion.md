title: "The Crypto Security Companion"
---

A 2-minute daily checklist for anyone holding crypto. Print it, bookmark it,
teach it to a friend.

## Before EVERYTHING

1. **Never share your [seed phrase](glossary/seed-phrase.md) or
   [private keys](glossary/private-key.md)** — with anyone, ever, anywhere. No
   exception. No support agent, no "official," no emergency.
2. **Type the domain yourself** for every wallet, exchange, and dApp. Phishing
   copies are one character off.
3. **Pause when money moves.** Scams live in urgency. A five-minute pause
   beats any password.

## Your wallet setup

- [ ] Seed phrase backed up **offline** (paper/metal), two safe locations.
- [ ] [Cold wallet](glossary/cold-wallet.md) holds long-term savings.
- [ ] [Hot wallet](glossary/hot-wallet.md) holds only daily spending.
- [ ] Test transfer done before large amounts.
- [ ] Wallet software and extensions kept updated.

## Before each transaction

- [ ] Address matches: first + last 6 characters.
- [ ] Network matches the receiver's expectation.
- [ ] **Test amount** sent and confirmed first.
- [ ] Fee (gas) checked before confirming.

## Red flags (walk away)

- "Connect your wallet to claim" airdrops
- "Support" asking for keys or a fee
- Celebrity/CEO DMs promising giveaways
- Tokens you can buy but "can't sell yet" ([rug pull](glossary/rug-pull.md),
  [honeypot](glossary/honeypot.md))
- Anything phrased as *urgent* or *now or never*

## The golden sentence

> If it's free money being offered to you by a stranger on the internet,
> you're the product — they want *your* money.
