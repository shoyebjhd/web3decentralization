About the Author

This site, Web3Decentralization, is written and maintained by Alex Vance, an independent researcher focused on the practical measurement of blockchain decentralization.

What I Do

I build open, methodology-first research tools and write about the underlying question this whole site is named after: how decentralized is a network, really? The flagship output is the Web3 Decentralization Intelligence Terminal — a free, interactive tool that scores major Layer-1 networks on decentralization and stress-tests their resilience.

Research Approach

  • Quantitative, not vibes: I measure decentralization with the Nakamoto Coefficient — how many independent parties would need to coordinate to halt or chain the network.
  • Four pillars: Composite scores combine infrastructure, capital, governance, and software client diversity, weighted 30 / 25 / 25 / 20.
  • Cited, public data: every calculation uses publicly available, sourced data (see the methodology page).
  • Stress-testing: the tool simulates AWS, GCP, and ISP-level outages to show how resilient each network is to infrastructure concentration.

Coverage

Per-chain decentralization audits for Bitcoin, Ethereum, Solana, Cardano, Avalanche, Polkadot, Cosmos Hub, and XRP Ledger, with ongoing updates. Try the live dashboard for any chain: open the Terminal.
