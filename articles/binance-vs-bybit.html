Fees: Binance vs Bybit

On spot, both charge an identical 0.10% maker and taker fee. Binance shaves this to 0.075% when you pay fees with BNB; Bybit offers partner-level fee discounts via its token and launchpool-related campaigns but no permanent universal discount on spot.

On futures, the two are extremely close: Binance is 0.02% maker / 0.04% taker; Bybit is 0.02% maker / 0.055% taker. At high frequency the difference is marginal — roughly a few dollars per $1,000,000 in notional traded.

Verdict: Nearly a tie; Binance has a slight edge with BNB discounts and a lower taker rate on futures.

Coin Selection and Listing Speed

Binance supports over 600 cryptocurrencies, generally the widest listing net and the most new-token launches. Bybit supports 400+, still far above Kraken or Coinbase, but trails Binance on the long tail of memecoins and niche projects.

Both run launchpads: Binance Launchpad uses a BNB commitment lottery; Bybit Launchpad uses a Bybit Points / token-hold model. Bybit historically opened new-perp listings quickly, making it popular with traders chasing newly listed derivatives.

Verdict: Binance for breadth, Bybit for perps listing speed.

Derivatives and Trading Experience

Binance Futures is the largest derivatives venue globally by open interest. It offers USDT-margined and coin-margined perpetuals, delivery contracts, trailing stops, TWAP, and a robust portfolio-margin mode. The interface is powerful but dense.

Bybit's derivatives engine is designed from the ground up for futures traders — the terminal layout, one-click risk tools, and position managers are consistently praised as cleaner than Binance's. Its V5 architecture gives fast routing, and its copy-trading product lets you mirror vetted strategy providers.

Verdict: Bybit for a futures-first, cleaner workflow; Binance for maximum instrument depth and options.

Security and Trust

Both are centralized exchanges with insurance funds and cold storage. Binance's SAFU fund, monthly Proof of Reserves, and $4.3B 2023 U.S. settlement are the relevant facts — post-settlement compliance has been substantially overhauled under new CEO Richard Teng. Bybit has a cleaner regulatory record, is licensed in Dubai (VARA) and Australia (AUSTRAC), publishes quarterly Proof of Reserves, and maintains a derivatives insurance reserve.

No major user-fund hot-wallet breach has been confirmed at either platform. In practice, both are considered large, well-run venues — but Binance carries more ongoing regulatory attention given its history.

Verdict: Bybit edges ahead on regulatory history; Binance is larger but under more scrutiny.

Geographic Availability

Binance is restricted in the United States (Binance.US is a separate, smaller entity), UK, Ontario, and Japan, among others. Bybit is not available in the United States (it exited the U.S. market in 2023) but covers most of the rest of the world, including a strong Dubai regional hub. European access for Bybit has tightened with MiCA; always confirm current availability in your jurisdiction before depositing.

Verdict: Neither fully serves the U.S.; overseas, availability is comparable with regional differences.

Customer Support

Binance's support is functional but frequently criticized for slow reaction times during high-load periods, with an AI-first ticketing system. Bybit is widely rated more responsive, with 24/7 live chat and quicker human escalation in most markets.

Verdict: Bybit.

Binance vs Bybit: Which Should You Choose?

  • Choose Binance if you want the widest coin selection, the biggest ecosystem (Earn, Launchpad, NFT, P2P, Card), lowest taker futures fee, and don't mind a denser interface.
  • Choose Bybit if you're derivatives-first, value a clean trading terminal and quick support, or want best-in-class copy trading with a strong regulatory track record.
  • For beginners: Bybit's interface is easier to parse; Binance has more beginner tutorials and a larger educational library.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Bybit safer than Binance?

Both maintain cold storage and insurance funds. Bybit has a cleaner regulatory history, while Binance has faced and addressed major U.S. enforcement action in 2023. Neither has confirmed a user-funds hot-wallet breach.

Does Bybit or Binance have lower fees?

Spot fees are identical at 0.10%. Binance offers a 0.075% spot rate with BNB and a lower futures taker fee (0.04% vs 0.055%). Bybit is competitive but slightly higher on futures taker fees.

Can I use both Binance and Bybit?

Yes. Many traders keep a primary exchange for spot/ecosystem features and a secondary for derivatives or specific listings. Diversifying counterparties across two large venues is a common risk-management approach.

Which exchange is banned in the US?

Both restrict U.S. users: Binance offers the separate, limited Binance.US; Bybit is not available in the United States at all.

Final Verdict

This is a genuine toss-up decided by workflow rather than quality — both are elite exchanges. You can't go far wrong with either.
