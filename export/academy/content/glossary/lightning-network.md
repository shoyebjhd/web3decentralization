The Lightning Network is Bitcoin's **state-channel** network for instant, near-free payments: open a channel once on-chain, then send unlimited **sats** off-chain, settling only when you close. Payments route across the network like packets.

## Why it matters

Lightning is how Bitcoin scales to coffee money without touching base-layer throughput — millions of instant payments, fees of a fraction of a cent. The honest caveats: channel liquidity management, needing to be online (or use watchtowers), and routing failures on large amounts. It's improving fast and already settles real volume.
