Account abstraction turns wallets themselves into programmable [smart contracts](https://web3decentralization.com/glossary/smart-contract/): spending limits, session keys for games, automatic recurring payments, gas paid in any token, and **recovery** without seed phrases. Ethereum's ERC-4337 is the live standard.

## Why it matters

Today's wallets are 1990s technology wearing a modern skin — one key, total power, zero forgiveness. Abstraction is how crypto UX finally grows up: bank-like safety rails (limits, recovery, fraud checks) without reintroducing banks. Watch it closely; whoever owns the default smart wallet owns the next hundred million users' habits.
