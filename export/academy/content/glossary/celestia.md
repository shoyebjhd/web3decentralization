Celestia is the first production modular data-availability layer: it orders transactions and guarantees their availability (via **data-availability sampling**) while doing zero execution — rollups plug in for cheap, scalable DA instead of posting everything to Ethereum.

## How it works

Namespaces separate each rollup's data; light nodes sample availability; [Tendermint](https://web3decentralization.com/glossary/tendermint/)-style consensus finalizes quickly. A rollup on Celestia gets Ethereum-independent scaling with light-client verifiability intact.

## Why it matters for decentralization

It unbundles the blockchain: execution anywhere, availability guaranteed by a purpose-built decentralized network — real competition for Ethereum's DA monopoly. The audit question moves with it: Celestia's validator set and sampling honesty become load-bearing for every chain built on top.

## Risks & trade-offs

Young network, young validator economics; sovereign-rollup tooling still maturing; no Ethereum settlement inheritance (sovereign security, not shared); and the existential bet that DA specialization wins over integrated designs.

## FAQ

**Celestia vs Ethereum DA?** Cheaper and purpose-built vs battle-tested and settlement-coupled. Sovereign rollups pick Celestia; Ethereum-aligned ones stay home.

**Do I need TIA?** To pay for blob space or stake, yes. To *use* apps built on it, no — abstraction hides it, as always.

**Main risk?** Validator-set youth plus everything-built-on-top leverage. The standard new-base-layer discount applies.
