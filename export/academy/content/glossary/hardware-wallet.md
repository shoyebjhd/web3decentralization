A hardware wallet is a dedicated offline device (Ledger, Trezor, and peers) that generates and stores your [private keys](https://web3decentralization.com/glossary/private-key/) in a secure chip, signing transactions without the keys ever touching an internet-connected computer. You verify details on its screen and approve with physical buttons.

## Why it matters

It's the default recommendation for meaningful holdings because it kills entire attack classes at once: clipboard malware, fake wallet apps, and remote hackers all fail against keys that never leave the device. Buy only from the manufacturer, generate the seed on-device, and it becomes the vault your [cold storage](https://web3decentralization.com/glossary/cold-wallet/) strategy is built on.
