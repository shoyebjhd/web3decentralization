The priority fee ("tip") is the optional extra you pay validators to jump the queue — on top of the burned [base fee](https://web3decentralization.com/glossary/base-fee/). Set it high in a gas war (NFT mints, liquidations); set it near zero when the chain is quiet and you'll still confirm.

## Why it matters

Tips are where MEV-adjacent games play out: searchers outbid you, wallets overpay by default, and during manias the tip dwarfs the base fee. Learning to set fees manually — base plus a sane tip — routinely saves frequent users more than any "gas token" gimmick ever will.
