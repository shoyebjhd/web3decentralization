Volatility is how much an asset's price jumps. Crypto is chronically volatile — double-digit percentage moves in a day are normal. High volatility is why crypto's upside is dramatic and why holding the full treasury in one coin is a gamble.

## Why it matters

Volatility shapes every strategy: stablecoins exist to escape it, swaps exist because someone is always willing to bet on it. It's also why "do your own research" and position sizing beat prediction — nobody controls the daily swings.
