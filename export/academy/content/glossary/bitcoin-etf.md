A Bitcoin ETF is a regular stock-market fund that tracks Bitcoin's price — buy it in any brokerage account, no wallet or keys needed. US spot ETFs (launched January 2024) hold actual BTC in institutional [custody](https://web3decentralization.com/glossary/self-custody/) and were among the most successful ETF launches in history.

## Why it matters

ETFs are the on-ramp for everyone who'll never touch a seed phrase — retirement accounts, advisors, institutions. The trade-off is philosophical and practical: you get price exposure with zero self-custody, zero censorship resistance, and full trust in the custodian. Adoption win, sovereignty loss — both true at once.
