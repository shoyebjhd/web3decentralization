The Superchain is Optimism's vision of many [OP Stack](https://web3decentralization.com/glossary/op-stack/) chains acting as one: shared bridging, shared sequencing, shared governance — [Base](https://web3decentralization.com/glossary/base-chain/), OP Mainnet, and others interoperating so smoothly that users stop noticing which chain they're on.

## How it works

Member chains standardize on the stack, plug into shared protocols for messaging and (eventually) sequencing, and participate in Collective governance. The endgame: cross-chain transactions as cheap and simple as same-chain ones, secured by Ethereum underneath.

## Why it matters for decentralization

Done right, it's cooperative scaling — shared security standards lifting all members. Done halfway, it's brand-centralization: one Collective, one sequencer design, one upgrade path governing an ever-larger share of L2 activity. The decentralization audit applies at Superchain scale, not just per chain.

## Risks & trade-offs

Governance capture by the largest members; monoculture risk (one bug class hits every member); upgrade coordination across sovereign chains; and the tension between shared standards and each chain's independence.

## FAQ

**Superchain vs Cosmos app-chains?** Opposite philosophies: Superchain shares standards/topology for unity; Cosmos shares nothing but protocols for sovereignty. Both scale; they centralize differently.

**Do I need OP tokens to use it?** No — usage needs only gas on whichever member chain you're on. Governance is the token's job.

**Is it live or vision?** Partially live (shared bridging growing, sequencing still per-chain). Grade the present, discount the roadmap.
