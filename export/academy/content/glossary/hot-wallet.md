A hot wallet is a wallet whose keys live on a device connected to the internet — a phone app, a browser extension, or a desktop program. It's convenient for daily spending and interacting with dApps, because signing a transaction takes seconds.

## Why it matters

Convenience has a security cost: anything connected to the internet is exposed to malware and phishing. The common pattern is small amounts for everyday use in a hot wallet, and the bulk of funds in a [cold wallet](https://web3decentralization.com/glossary/cold-wallet/).
