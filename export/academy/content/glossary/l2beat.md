L2Beat (l2beat.com) is the independent risk dashboard for Ethereum scaling: every major rollup graded on stages (0/1/2 decentralization maturity), operator powers, upgrade keys, and what's actually verified vs claimed. The industry's shared source of uncomfortable truth.

## How it works

Researchers dissect each project's contracts and permissions, publishing per-project risk cards: who can upgrade, who sequences, exit windows, data availability, and a maturity stage. Stage 2 (fully decentralized, trustless) remains nearly unoccupied — the dashboard's most Markus-important column.

## Why it matters for decentralization

Marketing claims "decentralized L2"; L2Beat shows the multisig signers. Before depositing anywhere, its risk tab answers the only questions that matter: who can take my money, freeze the chain, or upgrade the rules — with names, thresholds, and time delays. Bookmark it next to the block explorer.

## Risks & trade-offs (of the tool itself)

Coverage lags brand-new launches; grades simplify genuinely complex designs; and Stage definitions evolve (yesterday's Stage 1 is today's flag). Use it as the starting audit, then verify the contracts yourself for size.

## FAQ

**What do Stages mean?** Stage 0: full operator control. Stage 1: fraud/validity proofs live with training wheels (council override). Stage 2: trustless, council only for bugs. Almost everything is Stage 0–1.

**Is L2Beat neutral?** Research-funded public good with transparent methodology — the closest thing scaling has to an auditor of record. Verify anyway; that's the ethos.

**Which tab first?** Risk. Always risk. Then TVL (size of the honeypot), then activity (is anyone actually using it?).
