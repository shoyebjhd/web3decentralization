Collateral is crypto you lock up to back a loan or a minted asset. DeFi lending is over-collateralized by default: deposit $150 of ETH to borrow $100 of stablecoins, because no bank is coming to collect if you default — the contract just sells your collateral.

## Why it matters

Collateral quality decides whether DeFi lending survives a crash. Volatile collateral + falling prices = cascading **liquidations**, which is how "safe" protocols blow up in a weekend. Always know what's backing what you're holding — especially "stable" assets.
