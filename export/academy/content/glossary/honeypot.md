A honeypot is a token contract that *looks* buyable but can't be sold — the contract's logic blocks sales, often silently, so you can buy in but never get your money back. It's the sink trap of crypto scams.

## Why it matters

Honeypots are invisible to beginners: the chart pumps, the buy works, the "contract verified" — and then sells fail. Tools like Honeypot.is scan the functions and flag them. Rule: if it's easy to buy but impossible to sell, the liquidity is a honeypot.
