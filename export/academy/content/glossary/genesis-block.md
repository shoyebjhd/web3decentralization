The genesis block is a blockchain's very first block — block #0 (and #1 on Bitcoin, which famously embedded a headline in it). It's baked into every node's software, so the whole chain anchors to it.

## Why it matters

The genesis block is the root of all hash links, so it can never change or be re ordered. It also reveals launch philosophy — pre-mines, hard-coded allocations, and who got tokens at the start are etched at block 0, which permanently shapes how decentralized a network's ownership is.
