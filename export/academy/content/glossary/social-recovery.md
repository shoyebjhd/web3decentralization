Social recovery replaces the lone [seed phrase](https://web3decentralization.com/glossary/seed-phrase/) with a circle of trusted people or devices: lose access, and a threshold of your "guardians" (friends, family, a second device) together authorize a new key. No single guardian can steal anything alone.

## Why it matters

Seed phrases fail ordinary humans — lost paper ends ownership permanently. Social recovery trades cryptographic purity for human reality: keys you can actually recover, at the cost of trusting your circle not to collude. Done with widely separated, non-communicating guardians, it's the most promising path to mass [self-custody](https://web3decentralization.com/glossary/self-custody/) that doesn't end in tears.
