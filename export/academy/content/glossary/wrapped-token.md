A wrapped token is a 1:1 IOU that lets a coin work on a chain it wasn't built for — WBTC is Bitcoin "wrapped" as an Ethereum token, locked BTC in custody backing each one. It unlocks cross-chain DeFi for otherwise stranded assets.

## Why it matters

Wrapping reintroduces exactly the trust crypto tries to remove: WBTC is only as good as its custodian's honesty and solvency. Every wrapped asset is a [bridge](https://web3decentralization.com/glossary/bridge/)-grade trust assumption wearing a familiar ticker — verify the custodian and the mint/burn transparency before treating it like the real thing.
