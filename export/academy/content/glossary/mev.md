MEV — Maximal Extractable Value — is the profit validators (and searchers) squeeze by reordering, including, or censoring transactions: front-running your swap, sandwiching your trade, or sniping liquidations. If your transaction sits visible in the [mempool](https://web3decentralization.com/glossary/mempool/), someone is pricing how to exploit it.

## Why it matters

MEV is a hidden tax on every user and a centralization engine: sophisticated operators out-earn honest validators, concentrating stake and builder power. It's also why private mempools, encrypted transactions, and proposer-builder separation exist — the ecosystem's immune response to its own transparency.
