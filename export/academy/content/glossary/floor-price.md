The floor price is the cheapest listed item in an NFT collection — the headline number quoted as "the collection is worth X." It moves with the cheapest seller's mood, not with any fundamental, and thin collections swing violently on single sales.

## Why it matters

Floors are sentiment gauges, not valuations: wash trading inflates them, panic listings crater them, and most holders couldn't all exit anywhere near the floor (try selling 100 items into 3 bids). Never mistake the lowest ask for what your NFT is "worth" — worth is the best real bid, and usually lower.
