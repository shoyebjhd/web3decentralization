A custodial wallet is one where someone else holds the [keys](https://web3decentralization.com/glossary/private-key/) — an exchange account, a fintech app, an ETF. You log in with a password; they control the blockchain. Convenient and recoverable, exactly like a bank account, with the same counterparty risks.

## Why it matters

Custodial is how most people *start* (and how billions were lost at Mt. Gox, FTX, and others): the custodian can freeze, lend, lose, or be ordered to seize your funds. Use custodians as on-ramps, not vaults — buy there, withdraw to [self-custody](https://web3decentralization.com/glossary/self-custody/), and size what you leave behind by what you'd survive losing.
