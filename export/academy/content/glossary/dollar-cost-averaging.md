Dollar-cost averaging (DCA) means buying a fixed dollar amount on a fixed schedule — $50 every Friday — regardless of price. You automatically buy more when cheap and less when expensive, removing timing decisions (and timing emotions) entirely.

## Why it matters

DCA is the strategy with the best beginner track record for one reason: it makes volatility your employee instead of your boss. No charts, no predictions, no panic-selling the bottom — just accumulation through full cycles. Boring, automatic, and historically brutal to underperform by trading instead.
