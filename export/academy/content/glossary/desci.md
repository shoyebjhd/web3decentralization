DeSci (decentralized science) funds and coordinates research outside universities and publishers: [DAOs](https://web3decentralization.com/glossary/dao/) pool capital, fund labs, and tokenize IP (IP-NFTs) so discoveries trade transparently instead of hiding behind paywalls and grant committees.

## How it matters for decentralization

Science has a centralization problem (funders, journals, prestige cartels) that looks a lot like pre-Bitcoin money. DeSci's bet: open funding markets plus on-chain provenance produce faster, more replicable research. The honest version is early and small; the hype version sells tokens against cures.

## Risks & trade-offs

Tiny real output so far versus large token raises; IP tokenization legally murky across jurisdictions; expert evaluation doesn't decentralize well (who judges the science?); and long timelines vs crypto attention spans.

## FAQ

**Any real results yet?** Longevity DAOs (VitaDAO) funding actual lab work — real but early. Most DeSci tokens remain promises, not portfolios.

**How is IP handled?** Typically IP-NFTs representing rights, governed by token holders. Untested at scale in real courts — the central open question.

**DeSci vs traditional grants?** Faster, more open, less rigorous (so far). Complementary experiment, not replacement — yet.
