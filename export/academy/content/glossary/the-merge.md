The Merge (September 2022) was Ethereum's switch from [proof-of-work](https://web3decentralization.com/glossary/proof-of-work/) mining to [proof-of-stake](https://web3decentralization.com/glossary/proof-of-stake/) validation — the entire $200B+ network changing its security engine mid-flight without downtime. Energy use dropped ~99.9% overnight.

## Why it matters

The Merge proved blockchains can reinvent their foundations live — and it moved Ethereum's security debate from "who burns power" to "who holds stake." It didn't lower fees (a common myth) and it concentrated new power in staking pools, which is exactly the capital-centralization question W3D now tracks on Ethereum.
