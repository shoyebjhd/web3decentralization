A block is a batch of transactions bundled together and added to a [blockchain](https://web3decentralization.com/glossary/blockchain/). Every block carries a timestamp, the transactions included, and a cryptographic hash linking it to the previous block — which is what makes the chain hard to rewrite.

## Why it matters

Block size and the time between blocks decide a network's throughput — one reason Bitcoin (~10 minutes, small blocks) and Ethereum (12 seconds) handle browsing differently. Understanding "blocks" makes the terms TPS, finality, and congestion less mysterious.
