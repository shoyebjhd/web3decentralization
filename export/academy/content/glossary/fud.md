FUD — fear, uncertainty, doubt — is negative sentiment (earned or manufactured) that crashes prices: real hacks and bans, but also coordinated short attacks, fake screenshots, and recycled old news presented as breaking. Someone is very often positioned to profit from your panic.

## Why it matters

FUD works because crypto markets run on narrative leverage — thin books plus hair triggers. The defense is verification speed: check primary sources (official posts, on-chain data, reputable confirmations) before acting, and note who benefits if you sell. Real problems deserve exits; manufactured ones deserve patience.
