A bull market is the euphoric phase — months of rising prices, record [ATHs](https://web3decentralization.com/glossary/all-time-high/), mainstream attention, and everyone a genius. Liquidity floods in, dubious projects raise fortunes, and discipline evaporates precisely when it's worth the most.

## Why it matters

Bulls make money and destroy judgment in equal measure: the same rally that validates your thesis tempts leverage, concentration, and abandoning every exit plan. Professionals use bulls to take profits into strength and derisk custody; amateurs use them to learn about cycles from the wrong side. Enjoy it — with pre-written sell rules.
