A yield vault (Yearn pioneered them) is a smart contract that automates [yield farming](https://web3decentralization.com/glossary/yield-farming/) for you: deposit tokens, and the vault moves them between strategies — lending, staking, compounding rewards — while you hold a single vault token representing your share.

## Why it matters

Vaults turn expert-level farming into one click, auto-compounding gains you'd never harvest manually. But every strategy the vault touches is risk you inherit — a hack anywhere in the stack drains the vault. "Set and forget" works until the one strategy you didn't read about blows up.
