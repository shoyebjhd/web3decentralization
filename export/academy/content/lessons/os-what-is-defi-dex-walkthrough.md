[DeFi](https://web3decentralization.com/glossary/defi/) — decentralized finance — is banking software that runs on [smart contracts](https://web3decentralization.com/glossary/smart-contract/) instead of a bank. The most beginner-friendly example is swapping tokens on a [decentralized exchange (DEX)](https://web3decentralization.com/glossary/decentralized-exchange/). Let's walk through one trade, from a beginner's point of view.

## The DEX trade, step by step

1. **Open a DEX** (like Uniswap) from a domain you verified yourself. 2. **Connect your wallet** — the DEX asks to "connect," it *reads* your address.    It can't move funds yet. 3. **Approve** the token you're swapping — this grants the contract permission    to spend exactly that token. Tighten limits when you can. 4. **Swap.** You'll see the rate, the [slippage](https://web3decentralization.com/glossary/slippage/)    tolerance, and the [gas fee](https://web3decentralization.com/glossary/gas/). Confirm. 5. **Done.** The trade is on-chain, permanent, and yours — no middleman, no    closing hours.

## What you didn't need

No account, no [KYC](https://web3decentralization.com/glossary/kyc/), no credit check, no withdrawal freeze. Permissionless finance, in practice.

## What you're now responsible for

- **Smart-contract risk** — the code does exactly what the code says. Bugs and   exploits happen to huge protocols. - **Slippage & bots** — set conservative [slippage](https://web3decentralization.com/glossary/slippage/). - **Your own approval hygiene** — never approve tokens you don't understand,   and ideally use a fresh wallet for experiments. - **[Impermanent loss](https://web3decentralization.com/glossary/impermanent-loss/)** if you're providing   [liquidity](https://web3decentralization.com/glossary/liquidity/) — that's an advanced activity, not a   beginner one.

## Try it safely (recommended)

Many chains have **testnets** with free test tokens ([learn more](https://web3decentralization.com/glossary/testnet/)). Practice a few swaps there before ever putting real funds on a DEX — it's real DeFi, with nothing at risk.
