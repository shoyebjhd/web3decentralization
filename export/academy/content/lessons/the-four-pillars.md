## A repeatable lens for any chain

The W3D methodology scores every network across **four pillars**. You will internalize them here so the Terminal becomes readable:

1. **Consensus** — how agreement is reached and how expensive it is to attack: the Nakamoto Coefficient, validator/miner distribution, cost of a 34%/51% attack.
2. **Active Enforcement** — the ability to block, roll back, or modify history: upgrade rights, emergency powers, "safety" switches.
3. **Governance** — who may change the rules, the upgrade path, and how decentralized that decision-making actually is.
4. **Social / Community** — the invisible layer: how many independent developers, node operators and users would even run a contested fork.

Every pillar has a quantifiable core and a qualitative layer. The Terminal converts those into a single readable **grade (A–F)** with per-pillar sub-scores and transparent evidence links.

**Always ask:** "which pillar is this narrative actually about?" 99 percent of crypto debate improves when people name the pillar they are discussing.
