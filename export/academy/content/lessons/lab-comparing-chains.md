## LAB · Side-by-Side Comparison

Real analysis is comparative — absolute numbers mean little until you see your chain against alternative designs under the same methodology.

## Tasks

1. Open a session per chain and build a two-chain comparison of: Nakamoto Coefficient, consensus threshold, and enforcement powers.
2. Explain, in one paragraph each, why the stronger chain of the pair holds its edge.
3. Record the chains that score a **C or below** and the single pillar dragging each one down.
