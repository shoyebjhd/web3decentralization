## LAB 1 · Your First Network Scan

Time to stop reading and start inspecting. Below is the live W3D Terminal showing **Bitcoin**. Take three minutes and complete the mission.

## Your mission

1. Open every tab on the left sidebar (Overview, Consensus, Active Enforcement, Governance).
2. Note the **Nakamoto Coefficient** — the minimum number of entities needed to collude and censor Bitcoin.
3. Scroll through the explorer and identify the top mining pools and their ownership.
4. Click **Stress Test** on any chain and watch what happens to the grade under compression.
