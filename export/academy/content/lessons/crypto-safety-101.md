## Your keys, your coins

In crypto, whoever holds the **private key** controls the funds. There is no password reset, no fraud hotline, no "cancel transaction". The single most valuable habit you can build is rigorous key hygiene.

## The rules that keep people safe

1. **Seed phrases are cash.** Anyone with your 12/24 words controls your wallet. Never share them, never type them into a website, never photograph them.
2. **Verify addresses twice.** Malware can swap a pasted address at the last second. Always confirm the final address.
3. **Cold storage for savings.** A hardware wallet (offline device) for anything you intend to hold long-term.
4. **Trust no DM.** "Support", "validator", "giveaway" — an overwhelming share of crypto theft starts with a friendly message.
5. **Check on-chain, not on screenshots.** Anyone can fake a website or a balance. Look up contract addresses on the explorer.

**Only rule you must memorize:** real protocols never ask for your seed phrase — ever. If something feels urgent, it is a scam. Slow down.
