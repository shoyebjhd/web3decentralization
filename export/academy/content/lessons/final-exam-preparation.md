## Final Exam · Study Guide

Before the exam, confirm you can answer each of these without notes:

- **Define** the Nakamoto Coefficient and give its classic sabotage threshold.
- **Name** the four pillars and give one real metric per pillar.
- **Explain** why consensus decentralization alone does not make a network decentralized.
- **Distinguish** proof-of-work vs proof-of-stake failure modes under stress.
- **Apply** the four pillars to the network you graded best — and defend the grade.

The exam is 6 questions, multiple choice, auto-graded. A pass (60%+) together with completing every lab earns your certificate automatically. You may retake until you pass.

**Tip:** the certificate serial is on-chain referenceable — keep it. Recruiters and founders actually check it.
