## Not all crypto is equally decentralized

Every token calls itself decentralized, but decentralization is a **measurable property**, not a marketing word. Think of it as a set of questions:

- **Who writes the code?** One team, or many independent contributors?
- **Who runs the network?** Do a handful of validators control most of the mining/staking power?
- **Who can change the rules?** Is there a governance upgrade path, and who can trigger it?
- **Who can stop you?** Can any organization block your transaction, freeze your funds, or censor your app?

When one entity answers "me" to too many of these, the network is centralized under that entity — regardless of how many users it has.
