You've built the core of a crypto education: what a [blockchain](https://web3decentralization.com/glossary/blockchain/) is, how wallets and keys work, how to buy and send safely, how [networks](https://web3decentralization.com/glossary/consensus/) run, and what [DeFi](https://web3decentralization.com/glossary/defi/) actually is. Here's how to keep going without getting scammed or overwhelmed.

## The learning map

**1. Vocabulary (build it daily)** Browse the [glossary](https://web3decentralization.com/glossary/), one term a day. Every term links to its neighbors, so ten minutes a day compounds fast.

**2. Practice on testnets** Get comfortable with wallets, DEXs, and staking on [testnets](https://web3decentralization.com/glossary/testnet/) before using real funds. Experience with zero risk is the single best advantage you can give yourself.

**3. Verify everything** Use [block explorers](https://web3decentralization.com/glossary/block-explorer/) to check transactions. Investigate tokens before touching them. Read [tokenomics](https://web3decentralization.com/glossary/tokenomics/) with a skeptic's eye.

**4. Deep-dive by interest** - **Security:** the [self-custody](https://web3decentralization.com/glossary/self-custody/) and   [cold wallet](https://web3decentralization.com/glossary/cold-wallet/) paths. - **Investing logic:** [market cap](https://web3decentralization.com/glossary/market-cap/),   [volatility](https://web3decentralization.com/glossary/volatility/), [tokenomics](https://web3decentralization.com/glossary/tokenomics/). - **Technology:** smart contracts, [L2s](https://web3decentralization.com/glossary/layer-2/),   [rollups](https://web3decentralization.com/glossary/rollup/), [ZK proofs](https://web3decentralization.com/glossary/zero-knowledge-proof/). - **How it's really run:** take the **Decentralization Analyst** path — you'll   learn to score a chain's distributed power the way W3D does.

## Habits that separate safe learners from victims

- Move slowly; verify identities out of band; never share keys.
- Only ever act when *you* initiated the contact.
- Learn how to read one block explorer and one token checker inside out.
- Play with amounts you can lose on anything new.

## Where to go next
