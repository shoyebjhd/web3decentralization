Thesis: "A billion-user funnel pointed at young infrastructure — distribution solved, decentralization unproven." This is a preliminary profile, not a scored audit — no composite score is assigned until independent measurement is complete.

## Consensus & Architecture

Proof-of-stake validators with dynamic sharding for throughput, designed from the start for massive scale. The tech targets Telegram-grade load; the validator set's independence, geographic spread, and client diversity are still maturing relative to older networks.

## Tokenomics

TON's early distribution (original mining era plus foundation holdings) concentrates supply more than mature chains — the permanent on-chain fact every future decentralization score must price in. Staking yields attract validators, but stake follows existing holders.

## Ecosystem

Wallet bots, tipping, mini-app games (Notcoin-era phenomena), and payments inside Telegram — onboarding measured in tens of millions, mostly custodial at first touch. The open question is conversion: how many bot users graduate to self-custody and on-chain activity.

## Risks

- **Supply concentration:** early distribution overhangs every governance and staking metric.
- **Platform dependence:** Telegram's app-store standing and corporate decisions gate the funnel.
- **Young validator decentralization:** set size, client diversity, and geographic spread all early-stage.

## Audit Status: Preliminary

W3D has not yet published four-pillar scores for TON. Validator independence and supply-distribution evolution are the measurements to watch. Until measured, treat third-party grades as provisional.
